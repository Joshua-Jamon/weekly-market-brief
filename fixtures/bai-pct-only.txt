TAC Index Air Cargo Market Update - October 26, 2026

The global Baltic Air Freight Index (BAI00) increased +3.9% week-over-week, reaching +27.1% year-over-year gains.

Hong Kong (BAI30): gained +4.2% WoW to push it up to +28.0% YoY
Shanghai (BAI80): gained a more modest +2.9% WoW to leave it at +19.2% YoY
Frankfurt (BAI20): surged +13.7% WoW, pushing its gain to +48.8% YoY

Rising rates reflect peak season demand combined with constrained jet fuel supplies.
Air cargo demand rose 7.7% year-on-year in September 2026 while capacity expanded 2.7%.
